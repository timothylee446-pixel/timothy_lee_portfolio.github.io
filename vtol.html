Horizontal stabilizer

Wing simulations

The wings must hold the lifting force without fracturing or deforming and losing their airfoil shape. I considered loading cases for normal flight, climbing, and sharp banking. Lifting force per wing is the G load times the predicted plane mass (3062 g). All studies used SolidWorks FEA.

Empty wing (baseline)

With no inner ribs and no added mass, the simulation fails at the 3G loading case.

Wing v1

Variably spaced inner ribs maintain the structural integrity of the wing, at a cost of 43.47 g added mass per wing.
