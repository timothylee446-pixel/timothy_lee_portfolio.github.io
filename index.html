Mechanical engineering student at Northeastern University, building drones, grippers, and consumer products.

I'm a third-year mechanical engineering major at Northeastern University, from Boston, Massachusetts. I love throwing myself into new things, which has led me to hiking, camping, travel, competitive spikeball, table tennis, and other sports. At school I'm an active member of AerospaceNU and Rev, an entrepreneurship club.

My first co-op was at SharkNinja, working on corded vacuums. My proudest achievement there was inventing a patented component that greatly improved the performance of a headlining Shark product.

Next I want to learn as much as I can in industry, then take those skills out on my own and start a tech company.
